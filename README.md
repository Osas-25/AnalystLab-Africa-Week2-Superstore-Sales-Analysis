# AnalystLab Africa - Week 2: Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship - Week 2**.

The project uses the **Superstore Sales Dataset** to analyse sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and sales trends over time.

Microsoft Power BI was used to transform the dataset into an interactive business intelligence dashboard and executive summary that provide actionable insights for management decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most and least profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What recommendations can management implement to improve business performance?

## Key Findings

* Total sales were approximately **$2.30M**.
* Total profit was approximately **$286.40K**.
* Overall profit margin was approximately **12.5%**.
* The **West region** recorded the highest sales and profit.
* The **Consumer segment** contributed approximately **50.56% of total sales**.
* **Technology** was the strongest-performing product category in both sales and profit.
* **Furniture** generated approximately **$742K in sales** but had a relatively low **2.5% profit margin**.
* Several products recorded negative profit and require further investigation.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

1. **Review Furniture pricing and discounts** to improve its low profit margin.
2. **Investigate loss-making products** by reviewing pricing, discounts, shipping costs, and demand.
3. **Strengthen the South region** through targeted sales and marketing initiatives.
4. **Invest in High-Performing Categories**, which demonstrated strong sales and profitability.
